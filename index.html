Accepted CSV columns and import rules

Supports nflverse weekly stats with player_display_name, player_id, season, week, season_type, recent_team, opponent_team, and stat fields such as receiving_yards, passing_yards, rushing_yards, receptions, targets, passing_tds, rushing_tds, or receiving_tds. Also accepts player_name or player, and team instead of recent_team. REG rows only. Players are matched by a stable ID when present; otherwise name and team. Rows without required fields are excluded.

No odds or matchup context is inferred from these game logs. Imported data is user-supplied, not independently verified.

SAMPLE MODE — Fictional players and logs. No live odds or modeled probabilities.
